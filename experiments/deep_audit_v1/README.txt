FinCluster Deep Audit v1

Purpose: descriptive quality audit of the already-collected final_candidate_v1 dataset.
It does NOT collect timing measurements and does NOT modify raw/processed research data.

Place these files in:
  Research\experiments\deep_audit_v1\

Run from Research root:
  .\experiments\deep_audit_v1\deep_audit.ps1

Outputs are written to:
  Research\results\final_candidate_v1\deep_audit\

Important: this script does not automatically promote the dataset. Review its outputs before freezing FINAL TRAINING DATA.
